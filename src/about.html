Our Story

Founded in 2015, CampusBites was created with a simple mission: to replace boring cafeteria food with wholesome, vibrant, and affordable dining options right in the heart of the campus.

We understand the hectic life of a student—late-night study sessions, early lectures, and back-to-back classes. That’s why we focus on quick service without compromising on nutrition or taste.
